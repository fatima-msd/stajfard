The Odyssey

Map of a Voyage

Every great mentor was once an apprentice. This is the map of a voyage from the classrooms of Mashhad and Tehran to the academies he founded.

  1. Chapter I

    First Classrooms

    Teaching at respected institutes in Mashhad (Golestan, Jahad Daneshgahi, Rahpouyandegan) and Tehran (Iran–Europe, Shahid Beheshti, Afagh), where years at the whiteboard turned experience into craft.

  2. Chapter II

    University of Tehran

    MA in TEFL, graduating top of his cohort; researcher in adult education and the psychology of learning and teaching.

  3. Chapter III

    Certified to Teach the World

    Two TESOL certificates — from the University of Arizona and Shahid Beheshti University — and a TEFL certificate from RTEFL, UK.

  4. Chapter IV

    Keeper of the Exam Gates

    Official IELTS instructor endorsed by IDP, AELC (Australia) and Irsafam; official TOEFL instructor endorsed by the University of California; PTE and CELPIP instructor endorsed by Pearson and Paragon.

  5. Chapter V

    Top IELTS Teacher, 1403

    Named Top IELTS Teacher of the year 1403 (2024) by IELTS Tehran.

  6. Chapter VI

    Mentor of Mentors

    Teacher trainer endorsed by the University of Cambridge; runs TTC courses with TOT certification from IELTSDaily; supervisor at Golestan, Vandad, oTeacher and IELTS8Plus.

  7. Chapter VII

    Founder

    Founder and senior instructor of iScore9 Academy for international exam preparation, and founder and lead trainer of Asatid-e Farda (“Teachers of Tomorrow”) Academy, designing teacher-training courses and materials.

  8. Chapter VIII

    Today: MVA Academy

    Senior supervisor, instructor and member of the education & ideation team at MVA Academy. And the voyage continues…
